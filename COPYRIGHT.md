# 证据与版权说明 | Evidence and Copyright

## 证据边界

逐篇笔记以本地 PDF 全文和 Zotero 书目信息为主要依据。笔记是面向学习的结构化释义，不代替原文，也不保证覆盖附录中的每一项估计。涉及重要数字、量表、表格或识别假设时，应回到论文原文核对。

研究设计按其实际证据强度描述：概念论文、综述、定性案例、观察性研究、准实验与随机实验彼此区分。论文作者的理论解释不自动等于已经验证的机制，显著系数也不自动等于有效因果识别。

## Evidence boundary

The notes use local full-text PDFs and Zotero bibliographic records as their primary sources. They are structured paraphrases for study, not substitutes for the articles, and may not reproduce every appendix estimate. Consequential numbers, measures, tables, and identifying assumptions should be checked against the original publication.

Research designs are described according to their actual evidentiary strength. Conceptual, review, qualitative, observational, quasi-experimental, and randomized studies are kept distinct. An author's mechanism interpretation is not automatically demonstrated mechanism evidence, and statistical significance alone does not establish identification validity.

## 版权边界 | Copyright boundary

本仓库只发布原创的阅读笔记、综述、索引和模板，不上传论文 PDF、全文摘录或批量提取文本。论文版权归各自作者和出版机构所有。读者应通过学校图书馆、出版社或 DOI 链接获取原文。

This repository publishes only original notes, syntheses, indexes, and templates. It does not distribute article PDFs, full-text reproductions, or extracted corpora. Copyright remains with the authors and publishers; readers should obtain the papers through libraries, publishers, or DOI links.
