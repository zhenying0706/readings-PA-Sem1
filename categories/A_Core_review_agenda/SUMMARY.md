# A. Core review / agenda | 核心综述与研究议程

## 中文 Summary

这一组是整个41篇阅读库的“概念地图”。四篇文章共同反对把行政摩擦或AI治理简化为单一好坏判断，但各自解决不同层面的问题。

- Halling与Baekgaard把行政负担拆成“国家行动—执行与沟通—主观体验—个体后果—负担容忍—政策反馈”的因果链，并标出哪些箭头已有证据。
- Campbell、Pandey与Arnesen把红带和行政负担放回同一个“有争议规则”家族，提醒研究者不要把员工体验与公民体验、组织绩效与政治建构割裂。
- Mergel等人把公共部门AI研究从采用与原则讨论推进到实施，强调组织能力、团队、数据、训练、监督与人机协作。
- Roehl与Hansen提供善治价值矩阵：自动化同时产生协同、权衡和边界，其效果取决于决策情境，而不是技术标签。

### 累积性理解

四篇可以连成一条研究逻辑：先确定研究对象究竟是规则、负担体验还是自动化决策；再区分组织内部机制与公民端结果；随后分析实施能力；最后评估效率、公平、问责、透明等公共价值之间的权衡。它们都不是“某项技术/规则具有平均净效应”的因果论文，而是搭建概念、证据和研究设计的脚手架。

### 建议阅读顺序

1. Halling & Baekgaard：掌握行政负担的核心变量与因果链。
2. Campbell et al.：理解红带与行政负担的概念边界。
3. Mergel et al.：把AI问题转化为实施与组织问题。
4. Roehl & Hansen：用善治价值评估自动化的条件性后果。

### 可形成的研究问题

最有潜力的交叉点是：AI实施是否把组织内部红带转移成公民端行政负担；这种转移是否因组织能力、人工复核和申诉渠道而异；管理者如何在效率与公平/问责之间形成“负担容忍度”；这些态度是否最终改变真实流程与服务获得。

## English Summary

This category supplies the conceptual map for the full reading set. Halling and Baekgaard organize administrative burden into a causal chain and grade the evidence behind its links. Campbell, Pandey, and Arnesen reunite red tape and administrative burden as related lenses on contested rules. Mergel and colleagues shift public-sector AI research from adoption and principles to implementation capacity and organizational change. Roehl and Hansen evaluate automated decisions through synergies, trade-offs, and limits across nine good-governance values.

Read together, the papers suggest a cumulative workflow: define whether the object is a rule, an experienced burden, or an automated decision; connect internal organizational mechanisms to citizen outcomes; examine implementation capacity; then evaluate conditional public-value trade-offs. None estimates a single average effect of “AI” or “rules.” Their contribution is to specify constructs, mechanisms, evidence gaps, and testable designs.

A promising integrated agenda asks whether AI implementation shifts internal red tape onto citizens as administrative burden; whether capacity, human review, and appeals moderate that shift; how officials form tolerance for efficiency–equity trade-offs; and whether those attitudes alter actual administrative procedures.
