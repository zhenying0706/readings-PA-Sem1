## 一、开场：30秒说明我读了什么 | 1. Opening: What I Read in 30 Seconds

**中文讲稿**

这41篇文献主要覆盖公共部门AI与生成式AI、组织实施与能力、官僚工作、问责与合法性、行政负担以及负担不平等。阅读以后，我认为这些研究不应分成“AI文献”和“行政负担文献”两组。它们可以连成一条共同的实施链：制度和公共价值决定AI怎样进入组织，组织实施重新分配官僚裁量，新的国家—公民界面产生或转移行政负担，负担进一步影响服务获得、公平、信任和合法性。

**English script**

The 41 studies cover public-sector and generative AI, organizational implementation and capacity, bureaucratic work, accountability and legitimacy, administrative burden, and burden inequality. After reviewing them, I do not think they should be separated into an AI literature and an administrative-burden literature. They form a common implementation chain. Institutions and public values shape how AI enters organizations; organizational implementation redistributes bureaucratic discretion; the resulting citizen–state interface creates, reduces, or transfers administrative burdens; and those burdens affect service access, equity, trust, and legitimacy.

**中文讲稿**

> 我把八个原始分类重组为五个问题模块。第一，概念基础解释行政负担、红带和公共价值。第二，生成式AI与实施能力研究AI如何从采用进入真实组织使用。第三，官僚工作与治理研究裁量迁移、问责和申诉。第四，行政负担与不平等研究成本由谁承担、公共价值由谁获得。第五，新加坡案例将实施、政策能力和监管纠错连接到亚太制度环境。五个模块最终回答同一个问题：AI进入公共部门以后，怎样重新分配裁量、工作和成本？

**English script**

> I reorganize the eight original categories into five problem-oriented modules. The first develops the conceptual foundations of administrative burden, red tape, and public value. The second examines how generative AI and implementation capacity move technology from adoption to actual organizational use. The third focuses on bureaucratic work and governance, especially the relocation of discretion, accountability, and appeal. The fourth examines who bears administrative costs and who receives public value. The fifth uses Singapore to connect implementation, policy capacity, and regulatory correction to an Asia-Pacific institutional context. Together, the five modules address one question: after AI enters the public sector, how does it redistribute discretion, labor, and costs?

## 三、第二张图：4分钟讲六个核心认识 | 3. Figure 2: Six Core Insights in Four Minutes

打开 / Open [图2：整合机制 / Figure 2: Integrated mechanism](ADVISOR_VISUAL_FRAMEWORKS.md#图2整合机制ai必须穿过实施链才会影响公民结果)。

### 认识一：AI不是一个稳定的“处理” | Insight 1: AI Is Not a Stable Treatment

**中文**：采用意愿、是否使用、使用频率、人机协作流程和自动决策是不同结果。组织能力、任务类型、正式授权、数据和监督决定技术在实践中究竟是什么。

**English**: Adoption intention, any use, frequency of use, human–machine collaboration, and automated decision-making are distinct outcomes. Organizational capacity, task type, formal authorization, data, and oversight determine what the technology becomes in practice.

### 认识二：裁量没有消失，只是迁移 | Insight 2: Discretion Moves Rather Than Disappears

**中文**：面对面判断减少以后，裁量会进入问题定义、数据、模型阈值、界面、异常处理和人工复核。分析单位应当是完整的社会技术工作系统，而不是孤立算法。

**English**: When face-to-face judgment declines, discretion moves into problem definition, data, model thresholds, interfaces, exception handling, and human review. The appropriate unit of analysis is the full sociotechnical work system, not an isolated algorithm.

### 认识三：行政负担是AI研究的重要公民端结果 | Insight 3: Administrative Burden Is a Key Citizen-Side Outcome

**中文**：数字化可能减少搜索、重复证明和等待，也可能把身份验证、数据提供、错误纠正和申诉转给公民或第三方。因此必须追问：减的是谁的负担？

**English**: Digitalization may reduce search, repeated documentation, and waiting, but it may also transfer identity verification, data provision, error correction, and appeals to citizens or third parties. The central question is therefore: whose burden is being reduced?

### 认识四：效果受到多层资源制约 | Insight 4: Effects Depend on Resources at Multiple Levels

**中文**：认知资源、行政与数字素养、前线工作负荷、组织培训、跨机构协作和非营利帮助共同决定同一流程的实际结果。

**English**: Cognitive resources, administrative and digital literacy, frontline workload, organizational training, interagency coordination, and nonprofit assistance jointly shape the outcomes of the same process.

### 认识五：合法性不能由接受度代替 | Insight 5: Acceptance Cannot Substitute for Legitimacy

**中文**：公民愿意使用、官僚支持采用、模型可解释或政府发布伦理原则，都不能单独证明合法性。合法制度还需要可质疑、可复核、可申诉、可纠正和明确责任。

**English**: Citizens’ willingness to use a service, bureaucratic support for adoption, model explainability, or governmental ethical principles cannot independently establish legitimacy. A legitimate system must also permit contestation, review, appeal, correction, and clear assignment of responsibility.

### 认识六：负担和减负都是政治选择 | Insight 6: Both Burden and Deburdening Are Political Choices

**中文**：应得性、意识形态、目标群体形象、地方能力和政治注意影响哪些程序被保留、哪些群体获得帮助，以及哪些地方实施减负。

**English**: Deservingness, ideology, images of target groups, local capacity, and political attention influence which procedures remain, which groups receive assistance, and which jurisdictions implement burden reduction.

## 四、第三张图：3分钟说明真正的研究空白 | 4. Figure 3: Explain the Real Research Gap in Three Minutes

打开 / Open [图3：证据空白与研究议程 / Figure 3: Evidence gaps and agenda](ADVISOR_VISUAL_FRAMEWORKS.md#图3证据空白缺的不是更多态度调查而是连起来的数据)。

**中文讲稿**

> 现有研究已经较一致地说明组织情境、裁量迁移和行政负担的重要性。真正的空白不是再做一份一般接受度调查，而是缺少把组织正式授权、任务级真实使用、前线处理、公民完整服务漏斗和长期公共价值结果连接起来的多层纵向数据。很多论文的机制比证据走得更远：横截面中介不能确认时间顺序，政策文本不等于执行，点击和意愿不等于参与，领取人数也不能直接说明哪一种负担下降。

**English script**

> Existing research converges on the importance of organizational context, the relocation of discretion, and administrative burden. The main gap is not the absence of another general acceptance survey. It is the lack of multilevel longitudinal data connecting formal organizational authorization, actual task-level use, frontline processing, the complete citizen service funnel, and long-term public-value outcomes. In many studies, the proposed mechanism goes beyond the available evidence. Cross-sectional mediation does not establish temporal order, policy texts do not equal implementation, clicks and intentions do not equal participation, and recipient counts do not reveal which type of burden has declined.

## 五、最值得发展的三条研究方向 | 5. Three Promising Research Directions

### 方向A：AI实施是否重新分配行政负担 | Route A: Does AI Implementation Redistribute Administrative Burden?

**中文**：以某项数字公共服务为对象，同时记录组织内部工作、前线任务、公民时间与心理成本、第三方帮助和申诉。核心贡献是从“是否减负”推进到“负担在谁之间怎样重新分配”。

**English**: Study a digital public service while jointly recording internal organizational work, frontline tasks, citizens’ time and psychological costs, third-party assistance, and appeals. The contribution is to move from asking whether burden declines to examining how burden is redistributed across actors.

### 方向B：组织支持如何转化为任务级AI使用和公共价值 | Route B: How Does Organizational Support Become Task-Level AI Use and Public Value?

**中文**：连接组织AI政策、培训和监督，公务员任务日志，以及产出质量、工作负荷、裁量和错误。核心贡献是从采用意愿推进到采用后的公共价值实现。

**English**: Connect organizational AI policies, training, and oversight with public officials’ task logs, output quality, workload, discretion, and errors. The contribution is to move from adoption intentions to post-adoption public-value realization.

### 方向C：治理保障能否改善算法行政的合法性 | Route C: Can Governance Safeguards Improve the Legitimacy of Algorithmic Administration?

**中文**：利用分阶段上线或实验比较解释、人工复核和申诉，测量错误纠正、程序公平、信任和群体差异。核心贡献是把规范性的“负责任AI原则”转化为可检验的制度设计。

**English**: Use staggered implementation or experiments to compare explanation, human review, and appeals, measuring error correction, procedural fairness, trust, and group differences. The contribution is to turn normative responsible-AI principles into testable institutional designs.

## 六、建议主动问导师的五个问题 | 6. Five Questions to Ask the Advisor

1. **中文**：三条路线中，哪一条最符合Independent Study最终产出的期待？  
   **English**: Which of the three routes best fits the expected final output of the Independent Study?
2. **中文**：应以组织或公务员为主要分析单位，还是以公民服务流程和行政负担为主要结果？  
   **English**: Should the main unit of analysis be the organization or public official, or should the study center on the citizen service process and administrative burden?
3. **中文**：应先形成理论综述和研究模型，还是尽快选择国家、政策领域和可获得数据？  
   **English**: Should I first develop the theoretical review and research model, or move quickly to selecting a country, policy domain, and available data?
4. **中文**：亚太比较应成为主设计，还是先深入研究中国、韩国、新加坡或台湾中的一个情境？  
   **English**: Should an Asia-Pacific comparison be the main design, or should I first examine one setting in China, Korea, Singapore, or Taiwan in depth?
5. **中文**：AI实施、官僚裁量、行政负担、公共价值与合法性中，哪一个应作为主线，哪些作为机制和结果？  
   **English**: Which concept should provide the main line of the project: AI implementation, bureaucratic discretion, administrative burden, public value, or legitimacy? Which should serve as mechanisms and outcomes?

## 七、导师可能追问及回答 | 7. Likely Advisor Questions and Short Answers

### “这41篇最一致的发现是什么？” | “What Is the Most Consistent Finding Across the 41 Studies?”

**中文**：AI后果不是技术能力直接产生的，而是由组织实施、人员裁量和制度保障共同形成。数字化也不是天然减负，它会改变成本和责任的承担位置。

**English**: AI outcomes do not result directly from technical capability. They are jointly shaped by organizational implementation, human discretion, and institutional safeguards. Digitalization also does not automatically reduce burden; it changes where costs and responsibilities are located.

### “现有研究最大的缺口是什么？” | “What Is the Largest Gap in Existing Research?”

**中文**：缺少跨层纵向行为数据。多数研究只观察组织、员工态度、公民选择或政策文本中的一个环节，很少连接正式授权、真实使用、前线互动和公民端长期结果。

**English**: The literature lacks multilevel longitudinal behavioral data. Most studies observe only one part of the process, such as the organization, employee attitudes, citizen choices, or policy texts. Few connect formal authorization, actual use, frontline interaction, and long-term citizen outcomes.

### “你的理论贡献可能在哪里？” | “Where Might Your Theoretical Contribution Lie?”

**中文**：可以把行政负担理解为AI实施产生公共价值的公民端传导机制，并把“技术采用”改写成“裁量与成本重新配置”。这样能连接AI实施、街头官僚和行政负担三个研究传统。

**English**: Administrative burden can be theorized as a citizen-side transmission mechanism through which AI implementation produces or undermines public value. Technology adoption can then be reframed as a reconfiguration of discretion and costs, connecting research on AI implementation, street-level bureaucracy, and administrative burden.

### “为什么这是公共管理问题？” | “Why Is This a Public Management Question?”

**中文**：核心结果不只是技术使用或系统性能，而是谁拥有裁量、如何组织责任、谁承担成本、怎样保障公平和申诉，以及公共价值能否实现。这些都是公共组织、政策实施和民主治理问题。

**English**: The central outcomes are not merely technology use or system performance. They concern who holds discretion, how responsibility is organized, who bears costs, how equity and appeal are protected, and whether public value is realized. These are questions of public organization, policy implementation, and democratic governance.

### “现有证据能否说明AI提高绩效或公平？” | “Does Existing Evidence Show That AI Improves Performance or Equity?”

**中文**：不能作一般性结论。少数实验和准实验识别了特定近端处理或局部效果，大量证据仍是案例、横截面关联和概念框架。更准确的结论是AI效果具有高度条件性，长期绩效和公平证据仍不足。

**English**: No general conclusion is warranted. A small number of experiments and quasi-experiments identify specific proximal treatments or local effects, while much of the evidence consists of cases, cross-sectional associations, and conceptual frameworks. The more defensible conclusion is that AI effects are highly conditional and that evidence about long-term performance and equity remains limited.

## 八、最后30秒总结 | 8. Final 30-Second Summary

**中文讲稿**

> 阅读这41篇以后，我认为最值得继续推进的不是问政府要不要用AI，而是问AI进入公共组织以后，裁量、工作和行政成本怎样在政府、公务员、公民和第三方之间重新分配；这种重新分配在什么能力和治理条件下改善绩效与公平，又在什么条件下制造新的负担和合法性风险。我希望下一步与导师确定一条主线、一个制度情境和一套能够连接组织与公民结果的数据。

**English script**

> After reading these 41 studies, I believe the most productive question is not whether governments should use AI. It is how discretion, work, and administrative costs are redistributed among governments, public employees, citizens, and third parties after AI enters public organizations. Under what capacity and governance conditions does this redistribution improve performance and equity, and under what conditions does it generate new burdens and legitimacy risks? My next step is to work with my advisor to select one primary theoretical line, one institutional setting, and a data strategy that connects organizational and citizen outcomes.
