# Halling & Baekgaard (2024)

[DOI / publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1093/jopart/muad023)

## 中文笔记

### 为什么做：本质动机

行政负担研究自2012年形成后迅速扩张，但“负担”有时指国家设置的客观程序，有时指公民的主观体验，有时又指不申领、健康受损等结果。作者认为，真正的问题不是再证明负担存在，而是把政策建构、执行过程、负担体验、负担容忍度和后果放进同一个因果框架，并检查各条箭头究竟有多少证据。

### Basic idea

用系统综述把碎片化研究拼成一张“行政负担因果地图”。核心判断是：既有证据大体支持负担具有后果、由政治与行政行动建构、且不平等地落在资源较少者身上；但前线服务、政府沟通、第三方和个人经历也会改变负担体验与负担容忍度。

### 方法设计

作者依照PRISMA流程，检索Web of Science及12本主要公共管理期刊，并通过参考文献追溯、联系活跃作者和学术网络补充工作论文。最终纳入2012年至2023年初的119篇研究，其中100篇同行评审论文、19篇工作论文。研究先封闭编码年份、方法、国家、政策领域与对象，再开放编码概念及因果关系，由两位作者反复校准。

### 证据支撑了什么

这不是一项新实验。它汇总的经验研究支持三项基础主张：行政负担会影响个体结果；其分布具有不平等性；国家行动会建构负担。综述进一步指出，负担体验并不总能由正式规则直接推出，前线执行、沟通、非预期行动和第三方均可能介入；个人经验、人格与流程结构会影响公众和决策者对负担的容忍。

### 研究空白与贡献

贡献一是建立整合性的因果模型，区分“国家行动—体验—结果—容忍—再建构”的不同环节；贡献二是标出强证据、弱证据与几乎未检验的联系。主要空白包括：国家行动是否因果性地改变主观负担；负担如何影响申领与民主行为；决策者的容忍度是否转化为真实政策；合法性、防欺诈与减负之间如何权衡。

### 未来可以做

优先做跨情境比较、深描公民体验的定性研究，以及能操纵规则或沟通方式的实验；把媒体、家庭、非营利组织等第三方纳入机制；追踪负担体验如何反馈到政治态度和政策改变。阅读时要记住：这是“证据结构图”，并不意味着每一条箭头都已获得同等强度的因果验证。

## English notes

### Why this paper: underlying motivation

The field had expanded rapidly since 2012, but “burden” was being used for state-imposed requirements, subjective experiences, and downstream outcomes. The authors therefore ask a more basic question: how do state action, implementation, experienced burden, burden tolerance, and outcomes fit together, and which proposed links are actually supported?

### Basic idea

The paper turns a fragmented literature into an integrated causal map. Existing evidence broadly supports the claims that burdens are consequential, constructed, and distributive, while also showing that frontline delivery, communication, third parties, and personal experience shape burden experiences and tolerance.

### Design and method

Following PRISMA, the authors searched Web of Science and 12 leading public-administration journals, traced references, and solicited working papers from active scholars. They included 119 studies from 2012 to early 2023: 100 peer-reviewed papers and 19 working papers. Closed coding captured setting and method; open coding identified concepts and causal relations, with repeated author calibration.

### What the evidence supports

This is not a new experiment. The reviewed evidence supports the three foundational propositions, but also shows that formal state rules do not mechanically determine experienced burden. Frontline behavior, government communication, unintended implementation, and third parties intervene; personal experience, personality, and process design shape tolerance.

### Gap, contribution, and next steps

The contribution is both integrative and diagnostic: a model connects construction, experience, outcomes, and feedback while distinguishing well-studied from weak links. High-priority gaps concern causal effects of state action on experience, effects on take-up and democratic behavior, whether policymakers’ tolerance changes actual policy, and legitimacy–burden trade-offs. Comparative, qualitative, and experimental designs should test these links and incorporate actors outside the immediate citizen–state encounter.
