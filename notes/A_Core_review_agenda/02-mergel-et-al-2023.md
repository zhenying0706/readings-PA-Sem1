# Mergel et al. (2023)

[DOI / publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1080/14719037.2023.2231950)

## 中文笔记

### 为什么做：本质动机

政府对AI投入很快，但公共管理研究长期偏向规范性讨论、技术盘点和伦理原则，较少解释AI如何在真实组织中被实施。作者要把研究重心从“政府是否采用AI”推进到“谁以何种组织安排、能力和治理方式让AI持续运作”。

### Basic idea

AI不是一个可即插即用的工具，而是一项重组工作流程、责任、技能和人机关系的组织变革。文章以特刊导论的方式综合多篇实施研究，并从知识、经验、方法与理论四个层面提出议程。

### 方法设计

本文不是独立样本研究，也没有统一统计检验。它先梳理公共部门AI实施的背景、风险与组织维度，再综合特刊中涵盖政策、组织与团队层级的案例和经验论文。例如，六个欧洲聊天机器人案例显示，实施需要新的AI团队、训练者角色、数据供给和持续监督。

### 证据支撑了什么

特刊论文共同表明，实施成败取决于组织情境，而非算法性能本身；人类仍承担问责，AI往往增强而非简单替代专业人员；数据、训练、跨专业协作、监督和可执行的伦理安排是持续使用的条件。本文本身不能据此给出跨政府平均因果效应。

### 研究空白与贡献

贡献是将“AI治理”重新落到公共管理的实施过程。作者区分四类空白：研究者与管理者的知识/能力缺口；技术进展快于经验积累的证据缺口；电子政务与公共管理之间的方法断裂；缺少能解释智能技术与组织共同演化的理论。

### 未来可以做

需要公共管理学者与计算机科学家合作，打开算法黑箱；用现场观察、纵向案例和实验研究管理者面对AI建议时的判断变化；比较不同自动化程度、政策领域和组织能力；检验伦理原则如何转化成例行程序，而不仅停留在原则清单。

## English notes

### Why this paper: underlying motivation

Government investment in AI was moving faster than public-management evidence. Much scholarship discussed adoption, ethics, or broad landscapes without showing how AI is implemented in real organizations. The paper shifts attention from whether government adopts AI to how organizational arrangements and capabilities make it work.

### Basic idea

AI is an organizational transformation, not a plug-in tool. It changes routines, accountability, skills, and human–machine relations. As a special-issue introduction, the paper synthesizes implementation studies and organizes the agenda around knowledge, empirical, methodological, and theoretical gaps.

### Design and evidence

The paper does not estimate an original effect. It reviews the special issue’s policy-, organization-, and team-level studies. One example—six European chatbot cases—shows the need for a new microsystem, AI-trainer roles, suitable data, training, and continuing supervision. Across the issue, implementation depends on organizational context; humans remain accountable; AI often augments rather than simply replaces public professionals.

### Gap, contribution, and next steps

Its contribution is to recenter AI research on implementation. The agenda identifies a capability gap, a widening empirical-evidence gap, a methodological divide between e-government and public management, and inadequate theory for coevolving technology and administration. Future work should combine technical and organizational expertise, use longitudinal and experimental designs, compare automation levels and contexts, and test how ethical principles become operational routines.
