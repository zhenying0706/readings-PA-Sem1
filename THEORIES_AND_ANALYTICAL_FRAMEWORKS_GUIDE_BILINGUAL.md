# 41篇论文理论与分析框架学习手册 | Bilingual Guide to Theories and Analytical Frameworks in 41 Papers

> 适用范围 / Scope: 41 papers in `Independent Study-Sem1`  
> 使用方法 / How to use: 每个概念先给中文解释，再给对应英文表达；逐篇地图用双语表格。Each concept is explained in Chinese and then in corresponding academic English; the paper map is bilingual.  
> 目标 / Goal: 不只记理论名称，而是能够复原因果逻辑、判断论文如何使用理论，并把构念转化为研究问题、变量、假设和识别设计。The aim is to reconstruct causal logic, evaluate how each paper uses theory, and translate constructs into research questions, variables, hypotheses, and identification strategies.

## 0. 先分清五个层次 | Distinguish Five Analytical Levels

### 0.1 四种理论使用等级 | Four Levels of Theory Use

- **直接检验 / Direct test**：理论生成明确命题，关键构念被操纵或测量，结果能支持或反驳该命题。The theory generates a directional proposition, key constructs are manipulated or measured, and the evidence can support or contradict the proposition.
- **部分或间接支持 / Partial or indirect support**：研究只观察理论链的一部分，或结果与理论一致，但关键中介、条件或替代解释没有被直接观察。Only part of the theoretical chain is observed, or results are consistent with the theory while key mediators, conditions, or alternatives remain unobserved.
- **解释性使用 / Interpretive use**：理论用于组织案例、编码材料或解释结果，但没有独立的理论检验。Theory organizes cases, codes material, or interprets results without an independent test.
- **议程或未检验 / Agenda setting or untested**：贡献在于概念、分类和未来命题，而不是已经证明某项效果。The contribution lies in concepts, classifications, or future propositions rather than demonstrated effects.

### 0.2 判断论文是否真正使用理论 | Six Questions for Evaluating Theory Use

1. 作者是否明确使用 `draw on`、`based on`、`through the lens of` 或 `grounded in`？Does the author explicitly ground the analysis in a theory?
2. 核心构念是否进入研究问题、编码、实验处理或回归变量？Do core constructs enter the research question, coding, experimental manipulation, or variables?
3. 理论是否产生方向明确的假设？Does the theory generate directional hypotheses?
4. 中介与调节是否真的被测量？Are mediators and moderators actually measured?
5. 结果是否也可能支持竞争理论？Could the same result support an alternative theory?
6. 论文是在检验既有理论，还是用理论语言建立新框架？Is the study testing an established theory or building a new framework?

只有“提到了理论”通常不够。Mention alone is insufficient. Strong theory use requires operationalized constructs or theory-derived propositions and an explicit treatment of mechanisms and alternatives.

## 2. 行政负担、红带与公民—国家互动 | Administrative Burden, Red Tape, and Citizen–State Interactions

### 2.1 行政负担理论 | Administrative-Burden Theory

**中文**

行政负担研究公民在政策实施中体验到的三类成本：学习成本是发现项目、理解资格和弄清流程；遵从成本是填写、提交、等待、出行、支付和持续证明；心理成本是压力、污名、失去自主、被不信任和权力不对等。基本链条是：

`国家行动与规则 → 执行、沟通和接触 → 三类成本 → 参与、获得、福利与信任 → 政策反馈`

三类成本不是三个量表分数的简单相加。同一要求可能同时产生多种成本，客观步骤也不等于主观体验。资源、前线人员、第三方和政策设计会改变规则转化为负担的过程。

**English**

Administrative-burden theory examines three costs citizens experience during policy implementation. Learning costs arise from discovering a program, understanding eligibility, and identifying procedures. Compliance costs include forms, documentation, waiting, travel, payments, and repeated verification. Psychological costs include stress, stigma, loss of autonomy, distrust, and power asymmetry. The basic chain is:

`State action and rules → implementation, communication, and encounters → three costs → participation, access, welfare, and trust → policy feedback`

The three costs are not merely additive scale scores. One requirement may generate several costs, and objective steps are not equivalent to subjective experience. Resources, frontline workers, third parties, and policy design condition how rules become burdens.

### 2.2 红带与行政负担的区别 | Red Tape versus Administrative Burden

**中文**：红带通常以组织成员或组织绩效为观察点，指不能有效服务合法目标却造成约束的规则。行政负担以公民—国家互动为观察点，关心公民付出的成本及其分配。一个规则可能对员工是红带、对公民是负担，也可能保护程序正义而不能简单删除。

**English**: Red tape usually begins from organizational members or organizational performance and refers to rules that impose constraints without effectively serving legitimate purposes. Administrative burden begins from citizen–state interaction and focuses on the costs citizens bear and their distribution. The same rule may constitute red tape for employees and burden for citizens, while still protecting procedural justice and therefore not being removable without trade-offs.

### 2.3 负担容忍、应得性与ordeal逻辑 | Burden Tolerance, Deservingness, and Ordeal Logic

**中文**：负担是否被保留取决于行动者是否认为成本正当。防欺诈、个人责任、资源稀缺和应得性常被用于支持程序。研究必须区分“负担存在”“行动者容忍负担”和“负担产生预期政策效果”。

**English**: Whether burdens are retained depends on whether political and administrative actors consider them legitimate. Fraud prevention, personal responsibility, scarce resources, and deservingness often justify procedures. Research must distinguish the existence of burden, tolerance of burden, and whether burden produces its intended policy effect.

## 3. 认知资源、启发式与行为机制 | Cognitive Resources, Heuristics, and Behavioral Mechanisms

### 3.1 人力资本、执行功能与稀缺 | Human Capital, Executive Function, and Scarcity

**中文**：规则要求注意、工作记忆、计划、抑制冲动和任务转换。贫困、疾病、压力与认知衰退既提高对公共帮助的需求，又削弱完成程序的能力，形成“人力资本Catch-22”。

**English**: Administrative procedures require attention, working memory, planning, inhibition, and task switching. Poverty, illness, stress, and cognitive decline simultaneously increase the need for public assistance and reduce the capacity to complete procedures, creating a human-capital Catch-22.

### 3.2 行政素养、资源保存与自我决定 | Administrative Literacy, Conservation of Resources, and Self-Determination

**中文**：行政素养是理解制度、导航程序、沟通和使用媒介的领域特定能力。资源保存理论认为资源损失或损失威胁产生压力；自我决定理论强调自主、能力与关系需要。行政素养可能通过保护自主和能力减少心理成本，社会资本则在个人资源不足时提供缓冲。

**English**: Administrative literacy is the domain-specific capacity to understand institutions, navigate procedures, communicate, and use relevant media. Conservation-of-resources theory predicts stress when resources are lost or threatened, while self-determination theory emphasizes autonomy, competence, and relatedness. Administrative literacy may reduce psychological costs by protecting autonomy and competence, and social capital may buffer insufficient individual resources.

### 3.3 有限理性、启发式与预期负担 | Bounded Rationality, Heuristics, and Anticipated Burden

**中文**：潜在申请者不会完整计算所有规则，而会用文件数量、预计时间和界面复杂度等显眼线索判断“是否值得开始”。因此负担可以在申请前通过预期产生退出。

**English**: Potential applicants do not fully calculate every rule. They use salient cues such as document counts, expected time, and interface complexity to judge whether an application is worth beginning. Burden can therefore cause exit through anticipation before formal application starts.

### 3.4 政治注意与有限资源 | Political Attention and Limited Resources

**中文**：政治注意是有限资源。领导关注可能调动预算、人员、协调和监督，但若这些中介未测量，注意只能被视为条件或解释，不能自动称为机制。

**English**: Political attention is scarce. Leadership attention may mobilize budgets, personnel, coordination, and oversight, but when these mediators are not measured, attention should be treated as a condition or interpretation rather than a demonstrated mechanism.

## 4. 街头官僚、系统层官僚与算法官僚 | Street-Level, System-Level, and Algorithmic Bureaucracy

### 4.1 街头官僚理论 | Street-Level Bureaucracy

**中文**：一线人员直接接触公民，面对资源不足、目标模糊、个案复杂和任务不可完全标准化，因此必须运用裁量与应对策略；日常决定由此成为实际政策。

`组织资源／规则／绩效压力 → 一线裁量与应对 → 公民待遇与政策结果`

**English**: Frontline workers interact directly with citizens under resource scarcity, ambiguous goals, complex cases, and tasks that cannot be fully standardized. They therefore exercise discretion and coping strategies, making everyday decisions a form of policy production.

`Organizational resources, rules, and performance pressure → frontline discretion and coping → citizen treatment and policy outcomes`

### 4.2 裁量迁移到系统层 | The Migration of Discretion to the System Level

**中文**：数字化不是消除裁量，而是把个案现场裁量迁移到数据定义、规则编码、模型选择、阈值、界面与异常处理。工程师、供应商、管理者、法务和数据团队因而成为决策网络的一部分。

**English**: Digitalization does not eliminate discretion. It moves case-level discretion into data definitions, rule encoding, model choice, thresholds, interfaces, and exception handling. Engineers, vendors, managers, legal teams, and data specialists therefore become part of the decision network.

### 4.3 算法官僚与四类数字裁量 | Algorithmic Bureaucracy and Four Forms of Digital Discretion

1. **问题定义 / Problem definition**：决定什么值得预测。What outcome is worth predicting?
2. **数据裁量 / Data discretion**：选择记录、缺失处理和偏差修正。Which records are included, and how are missingness and bias handled?
3. **模型裁量 / Model discretion**：选择目标函数、阈值和错误权衡。Which objective, threshold, and error trade-off are used?
4. **使用裁量 / Use discretion**：一线人员接受、修正、绕开或拒绝建议。When do frontline workers accept, revise, bypass, or reject recommendations?

**证据提醒 / Evidence note**：算法一致性不等于没有偏差；偏差可能从可见个案层转移到不透明系统层。Algorithmic consistency does not imply absence of bias; bias may move from visible case-level decisions into an opaque system layer.

### 4.4 工作负荷、认知负荷与歧视 | Workload, Cognitive Load, and Discrimination

**中文**：偏好型歧视源于负面偏好；统计型歧视在信息不足时用群体平均替代个体信息；隐性偏见来自自动认知或情感联结。高工作负荷可能强化后两类机制，因为时间压力增加对捷径和刻板印象的依赖。结果差异本身不能区分机制。

**English**: Taste-based discrimination arises from negative preferences; statistical discrimination substitutes group averages for individual information; implicit bias arises from automatic cognitive or affective associations. High workload may strengthen the latter two mechanisms by increasing reliance on shortcuts and stereotypes. Outcome differences alone cannot distinguish among these mechanisms.

## 5. 技术实践、组织设计与创新 | Technology in Practice, Organizational Design, and Innovation

### 5.1 社会技术与技术实践 | Sociotechnical and Technology-in-Practice Perspectives

**中文**：技术决定论写成“技术特征→组织结果”。技术实践视角认为，人们通过规则、规范、解释和能力把技术实施出来：

`技术属性 × 组织规则 × 行政文化 × 使用者解释 → 实际实践 → 组织结果`

**English**: Technological determinism reduces explanation to technology features → organizational outcomes. A technology-in-practice perspective argues that technology is enacted through rules, norms, interpretations, and capabilities:

`Technological attributes × organizational rules × administrative culture × user interpretation → enacted practice → organizational outcomes`

### 5.2 组织设计的四个问题 | Four Problems of Organizational Design

| 组织问题 / Design problem | 核心问题 / Core question | AI进入后的变化 / Change after AI |
|---|---|---|
| 任务分解 / Task division | 总目标怎样拆成子任务？How is the goal divided? | 收集、回答、升级、训练和监控被重新拆分。Collection, response, escalation, training, and monitoring are repartitioned. |
| 任务分配 / Task allocation | 谁承担哪项任务？Who performs each task? | 机器处理重复任务，人类处理例外、训练和责任。Machines handle repetition; humans handle exceptions, training, and responsibility. |
| 奖励 / Rewards | 如何激励行动者？How are actors motivated? | 新角色、认可、学习和问责影响合作。Roles, recognition, learning, and accountability shape collaboration. |
| 信息 / Information | 如何获得协调所需信息？How is coordination information provided? | 知识库、反馈、错误标注与跨部门数据流变得关键。Knowledge bases, feedback, error labels, and interdepartmental data flows become central. |

### 5.3 公共部门创新类型 | Types of Public-Sector Innovation

**中文**：AI可能构成行政过程、技术过程、产品/服务、治理或概念创新。创新程度取决于分析层级和比较基线。技术上激进的系统在组织日常中可能只是渐进维护；反欺诈工具也可能通过重新定义“可疑者”形成概念创新。

**English**: AI may constitute administrative-process, technological-process, product/service, governance, or conceptual innovation. Its degree of novelty depends on the level of analysis and comparison baseline. A technologically radical system may be incremental organizational maintenance, while an anti-fraud tool may produce conceptual innovation by redefining who counts as suspicious.

### 5.4 边界理论与组织学习 | Boundary Theory and Organizational Learning

- **边界对象 / Boundary objects**：不同群体可共同使用并各自解释的模板、标准、原型和案例。Artifacts such as templates, standards, prototypes, and cases that different groups can use while interpreting differently.
- **知识经纪人 / Knowledge brokers**：翻译术语、连接网络并帮助知识本地化的人。Actors who translate language, connect networks, and localize knowledge.
- **边界互动 / Boundary interactions**：会议、访问、共同项目和跨界实践。Meetings, visits, joint projects, and cross-boundary practices.

**核心判断 / Core claim**：`know-what`与`know-how`跨边界的难度不同。同伴论坛容易传实施诀窍，却未必建立理解模型原理和偏差的能力。Know-what and know-how travel differently. Peer forums may transfer implementation practice without building the capacity to understand model principles and bias.

## 6. 技术采用与持续使用 | Technology Adoption and Continued Use

### 6.1 TAM：技术接受模型 | Technology Acceptance Model

`感知有用性 + 感知易用性 → 态度／意向 → 使用`  
`Perceived usefulness + perceived ease of use → attitude/intention → use`

**中文**：TAM构念清楚，但容易把采用缩减为个人态度，忽略任务风险、授权、资源、权力与公共价值。必须区分使用意愿、是否使用以及使用频率与深度。

**English**: TAM offers clear constructs but can reduce adoption to individual attitudes while neglecting task risk, authorization, resources, power, and public value. Intention, any use, and frequency or depth of use must be distinguished.

### 6.2 信任、依恋与校准信任 | Trust, Attachment, and Calibrated Trust

**中文**：信任降低无法完全检查系统时的复杂性。应区分对基础设施安全、具体回答可靠性和部署组织纠错能力的信任。公共部门的目标不是信任越高越好，而是信任随任务、证据、错误成本和复核能力变化。

**English**: Trust reduces complexity when a system cannot be fully inspected. Trust in infrastructure security, specific output reliability, and the deploying organization’s capacity to correct errors should be separated. The public-sector objective is not maximal trust but calibrated trust that varies with tasks, evidence, error costs, and review capacity.

### 6.3 TOE：技术—组织—环境 | Technology–Organization–Environment

| 层次 / Dimension | 典型条件 / Typical conditions |
|---|---|
| 技术 / Technology | 兼容性、复杂性、数据、基础设施、相对优势、安全 / compatibility, complexity, data, infrastructure, relative advantage, security |
| 组织 / Organization | 规模、资源、领导、战略、技能、文化、协作 / size, resources, leadership, strategy, skills, culture, coordination |
| 环境 / Environment | 法规、供应商、伙伴、同业压力、政治监督、社会期待 / regulation, vendors, partners, peer pressure, political oversight, social expectations |

**阶段化问题 / Stage-sensitive question**：探索、试点、核心整合和规模化需要的条件不同。只把因素放入T/O/E三栏是分类；理论解释还要说明因素如何互动、在何阶段起作用以及通过什么机制影响结果。Exploration, piloting, core integration, and scaling require different conditions. Sorting factors into T/O/E is classification; explanation requires interactions, stages, and mechanisms.

### 6.4 TOI：技术—组织—个人 | Technological–Organizational–Individual

`技术设计 × 组织实施 × 个人资源 → 数字接触与行政负担`  
`Technological design × organizational implementation × individual resources → digital encounters and administrative burden`

**中文**：TOE适合解释机构采用；TOI适合解释公民数字服务体验。两者都是上层框架，具体因果箭头仍需其他理论。

**English**: TOE is suited to organizational adoption; TOI is suited to citizen experience with digital services. Both are umbrella frameworks whose causal arrows require additional theory.

### 6.5 创新扩散与隐藏创新 | Diffusion of Innovation and Hidden Innovation

**中文**：创新扩散通常认为相对优势、兼容性、可试用性和可观察性促进采用。公共组织中，高可观察性的风险AI可能因外部审查和失败成本而抑制采用：

`可见性↑ → 审查与失败成本↑ → 风险规避↑ → 采用↓`

**English**: Diffusion theory commonly treats relative advantage, compatibility, trialability, and observability as adoption drivers. In public organizations, observability of risky AI may instead deter adoption by increasing external scrutiny and political failure costs:

`Visibility ↑ → scrutiny and failure costs ↑ → risk aversion ↑ → adoption ↓`

## 7. 自动化、增强、自我效能与人机关系 | Automation, Augmentation, Self-Efficacy, and Human–AI Relations

### 7.1 自动化—增强悖论 | Automation–Augmentation Paradox

**中文**：自动化替代任务，可能减少裁量并造成技能退化；增强释放时间、扩展信息并可能提高判断质量。关键不是二者是否抵消，而是任务可编码性、错误成本、专业性、复核权、依赖强度与学习等边界条件。

**English**: Automation substitutes for tasks and may reduce discretion or degrade skills. Augmentation releases time, expands information, and may improve judgment. The central issue is not whether the two cancel out but which boundary conditions, including task codifiability, error costs, expertise, review authority, dependence, and learning, determine their balance.

### 7.2 主动资源配置 | Proactive Resource Allocation

`GenAI使用 → 时间与认知资源释放 → 资源转向核心任务 → 努力／绩效`  
`GenAI use → release of time and cognitive resources → reallocation to core tasks → effort/performance`

**证据要求 / Evidence requirement**：要证明资源重配，应直接观察时间、任务和资源去向；仅测依赖、自我效能与努力仍是间接解释。Demonstrating reallocation requires observing time, tasks, and destinations of released resources; dependence, self-efficacy, and effort alone provide indirect evidence.

### 7.3 社会认知与自我效能 | Social Cognitive Theory and Self-Efficacy

**中文**：自我效能是对自己能否完成特定行动的信念，影响目标、坚持、努力和恢复。AI帮助可能创造成功经验，也可能使成功被完全归因于机器。统计中介不能单独建立时间顺序。

**English**: Self-efficacy is the belief that one can organize and complete a specific action. It shapes goals, persistence, effort, and recovery. AI assistance may create mastery experiences, but users may also attribute success entirely to the machine. Statistical mediation alone cannot establish temporal ordering.

### 7.4 信号、算法厌恶与算法欣赏 | Signaling, Algorithm Aversion, and Algorithm Appreciation

**中文**：信息不对称时，人们从AI披露推断组织是否公平、创新或重视自己。算法厌恶常出现在独特、道德和高不确定任务；算法欣赏常出现在量化、可验证或人类偏差突出的任务。必须区分“得知用了AI”的披露效应与AI真实改变选择的部署效应。

**English**: Under information asymmetry, AI disclosure signals whether an organization is fair, innovative, or considerate. Algorithm aversion is common in unique, moral, and highly uncertain tasks; algorithm appreciation is more common in quantitative, verifiable tasks or when human bias is salient. The disclosure effect of learning that AI is used must be distinguished from the deployment effect of AI actually changing decisions.

## 8. 公共价值、善治与价值权衡 | Public Value, Good Governance, and Value Trade-Offs

### 8.1 公共价值是多维结果 | Public Value Is Multidimensional

**中文**：公共组织不能只最大化效率。相关价值包括问责、效率、平等、公平、韧性、回应性、隐私、法治、透明、参与、合法性与人权。自动化与价值之间可能是协同、权衡或技术无法独自解决的制度边界。

**English**: Public organizations cannot maximize efficiency alone. Relevant values include accountability, efficiency, equality, equity, resilience, responsiveness, privacy, the rule of law, transparency, participation, legitimacy, and human rights. Automation may create synergies, trade-offs, or institutional limits that technology cannot resolve by itself.

### 8.2 价值权衡怎样进入官僚选择 | How Value Trade-Offs Enter Bureaucratic Choice

**中文**：官僚具有采用裁量，会根据系统被描述的效率、公平和风险后果调整支持。但情境实验识别的是特定提示的态度效应，不能推出真实组织一定按同样方式部署。

**English**: Bureaucrats exercise adoption discretion and adjust support in response to information about efficiency, equity, and risk. Vignette experiments identify attitudinal effects of specific cues, not how organizations necessarily deploy systems in practice.

### 8.3 价值—风险—指标—保障矩阵 | Value–Risk–Indicator–Safeguard Matrix

| 价值 / Value | 风险问题 / Risk question | 指标 / Indicators | 可比较保障 / Safeguards to compare |
|---|---|---|---|
| 效率 / Efficiency | 是否减少总时间，还是转移工作？Does total time fall or work move? | 端到端时长、返工、员工与公民时间 / end-to-end time, rework, employee and citizen time | 自动化程度、人机分工 / automation level, work allocation |
| 公平 / Equity | 错误是否集中于群体？Are errors concentrated by group? | 假阳性/阴性、获得率、申诉成功 / false positives/negatives, access, appeal success | 审计、数据、人工复核 / audit, data design, human review |
| 问责 / Accountability | 谁解释、判断和纠正？Who explains, judges, and corrects? | 责任归属、日志、纠错时间 / responsibility, logs, correction time | 责任规则、独立复核 / responsibility rules, independent review |
| 隐私 / Privacy | 是否收集超出目的的数据？Is data collection excessive? | 最小化、二次使用、保留期 / minimization, reuse, retention | 同意、访问控制、最小化 / consent, access control, minimization |
| 回应性 / Responsiveness | 能否处理例外与反馈？Can exceptions and feedback be handled? | 人工升级、例外解决、反馈闭环 / human escalation, exception resolution, feedback closure | 混合渠道、异常处理 / hybrid channels, exception handling |
| 法治 / Rule of law | 能否知情、陈述、质疑和申诉？Can people know, speak, contest, and appeal? | 通知、理由、听证、申诉可及性 / notice, reasons, hearing, appeal access | 解释、程序权利 / explanation, procedural rights |

## 9. 问责、行政正义、隐私与合法性 | Accountability, Administrative Justice, Privacy, and Legitimacy

### 9.1 问责的三阶段 | Three Stages of Accountability

1. **信息与说明 / Information and explanation**：系统做了什么、依据什么、谁参与？What did the system do, on what basis, and who participated?
2. **审问与判断 / Questioning and judgment**：论坛能否挑战解释并依据规范判断？Can the forum challenge the explanation and judge it against standards?
3. **后果与纠正 / Consequences and correction**：能否纠正、赔偿、制裁、修改或停止？Can decisions be corrected, compensation or sanctions imposed, and systems changed or stopped?

**核心判断 / Core claim**：可解释AI最多解决第一阶段的一部分。没有判断能力和实际后果，就没有完整问责。Explainable AI addresses at most part of the first stage. Without judgment capacity and consequences, accountability is incomplete.

### 9.2 行政正义与正当程序 | Administrative Justice and Due Process

**中文**：行政正义关注决定是否依法、公平、可理解、可质疑，当事人能否陈述例外并获得补救。预测准确的系统仍可能缺乏正当程序；可解释的低质量系统也不会因“说得清楚”而合法。

**English**: Administrative justice asks whether a decision is lawful, fair, understandable, and contestable and whether affected people can present exceptions and obtain remedies. A predictively accurate system may still violate due process, while an explainable low-quality system does not become legitimate merely because it can articulate reasons.

### 9.3 隐私计算与隐私悖论 | Privacy Calculus and the Privacy Paradox

`感知效用 − 隐私风险与披露成本 → 披露或使用`  
`Perceived utility − privacy risk and disclosure cost → disclosure or use`

**中文**：隐私悖论是表达的担忧与实际行为不一致。情境选择并非现实授权；更强研究同时观察态度、选择、授权、撤回与日志。

**English**: The privacy paradox is the inconsistency between stated concern and behavior. Hypothetical choice is not actual authorization; stronger research observes attitudes, choices, authorization, withdrawal, and behavioral logs together.

### 9.4 四类公平与合法性 | Four Forms of Fairness and Legitimacy

- 分配公平 / Distributive fairness：结果和资源怎样分配？How are outcomes and resources distributed?
- 程序公平 / Procedural fairness：过程是否一致、中立、允许发声？Is the process consistent, neutral, and voice-enabling?
- 互动公平 / Interactional fairness：接触是否尊重并充分解释？Are interactions respectful and sufficiently explained?
- 纠正公平 / Corrective justice：错误发生后能否补救？Can errors be remedied?

程序一致性不等于程序复杂性。可以用统一规则配合自动取数，同时提高一致性并降低材料成本；没有必要以增加公民负担换取公平。Procedural consistency is not procedural complexity. Standardized rules can be combined with automatic data retrieval to improve consistency while reducing documentation costs; fairness need not require more citizen burden.

## 10. 代表性、社会认同、目标群体与交叉性 | Representation, Social Identity, Target Groups, and Intersectionality

### 10.1 代表性官僚 | Representative Bureaucracy

| 层次 / Form | 中文解释 | English definition |
|---|---|---|
| 被动代表 / Passive representation | 官僚队伍的人口构成是否反映公民群体 | Whether the demographic composition of the bureaucracy reflects the public |
| 主动代表 / Active representation | 具有某身份的官僚是否运用裁量改善该群体结果 | Whether bureaucrats use discretion to improve outcomes for the represented group |
| 象征性代表 / Symbolic representation | 共享身份本身是否提高信任、公平和合法性感知 | Whether shared identity itself improves perceived trust, fairness, and legitimacy |

**边界 / Boundary**：被动代表不会自动变成主动代表。还需要裁量、身份与政策领域相关、组织允许行动，以及个人愿意代表。Passive representation does not automatically become active representation; discretion, policy relevance, organizational permission, and willingness are also required.

### 10.2 机器中立启发式与竞争种族解释 | Machine-Neutrality Heuristic and Competing Racial Explanations

**中文**：公众可能把机器与客观、一致联系，即使数据和设计仍含偏差。种族威胁理论和白人特权理论对多数群体反应提出不同预期。使用竞争理论的价值在于让结果区分解释，但一个执法场景不能推广到福利、招聘和风险评估。

**English**: Citizens may associate machines with objectivity and consistency even when data and design contain bias. Racial-threat and White-privilege theories generate different expectations about majority-group responses. Competing theories improve explanatory leverage, but evidence from one enforcement setting cannot be generalized to welfare, recruitment, or risk assessment.

### 10.3 社会认同理论 | Social Identity Theory

**中文**：个人从群体成员身份形成部分自我概念，并据此解释组织信号与规范。性别等身份应被理解为社会化经验和制度位置，而不是“天然”技术偏好。若风险认知是机制，就应直接测风险、经验与身份显著性。

**English**: Individuals derive part of their self-concept from group membership and use it to interpret organizational signals and norms. Gender and other identities should be treated as socialized experience and institutional position, not innate technological preference. If risk perception is the mechanism, risk, experience, and identity salience should be measured directly.

### 10.4 目标群体社会建构与CARIN | Social Construction of Target Populations and CARIN

**中文**：政策设计受到目标群体的政治权力和社会形象影响。强大且被视为应得的群体更容易获得利益，弱小或负面建构的群体更容易承担制裁与负担。

| CARIN维度 / Dimension | 核心问题 / Core question |
|---|---|
| Control | 困境是否被认为由本人控制？Is the condition considered under personal control? |
| Attitude | 对社会或政府的态度是否被视为顺从和感恩？Is the group seen as compliant and grateful? |
| Reciprocity | 是否曾贡献或将回报社会？Has the group contributed or will it reciprocate? |
| Identity | 与评价者的社会距离是否近？How socially close is the group to the evaluator? |
| Need | 需求是否严重？How severe is the need? |

**English summary**: Policy design is shaped by a target group’s political power and social image. Powerful and positively constructed groups are more likely to receive benefits, while weak or negatively constructed groups are more likely to bear sanctions and burdens.

### 10.5 意识形态与动机性推理 | Ideology and Motivated Reasoning

**中文**：行动者可能从身份、价值或期望结论出发，选择性接受支持自己的理由。横截面态度不能证明意识形态造成执行行为。更强设计可随机化欺诈率、目标群体、公平后果和程序效果，并连接实际帮助或拒绝。

**English**: Actors may begin from identity, values, or desired conclusions and selectively accept supporting reasons. Cross-sectional attitudes do not show that ideology causes implementation behavior. Stronger designs can randomize fraud rates, target groups, equity consequences, and procedural effects and connect responses to actual assistance or denial.

### 10.6 女性主义国家、性别化组织、再生产正义与交叉性 | Feminist State, Gendered Organizations, Reproductive Justice, and Intersectionality

**中文**

- 女性主义国家理论认为国家制度会塑造谁承担照护、谁能控制身体、谁获得权利。
- 性别化组织理论指出，看似中性的时间、证明和岗位规则可能以无照护责任的理想劳动者为默认。
- 再生产正义不仅关心终止妊娠权，也关心生育、抚养和安全生活的实质能力。
- 交叉性强调性别与种族、阶级、残障、移民等共同构成制度位置，而不是独立主效应的简单相加。

**English**

- Feminist-state theory examines how state institutions allocate care, bodily autonomy, and rights.
- Gendered-organization theory shows how apparently neutral rules about time, proof, and work may assume an ideal worker without care obligations.
- Reproductive justice concerns not only termination rights but substantive capacities to have children, raise them, and live safely.
- Intersectionality treats gender, race, class, disability, migration status, and other identities as jointly constituting institutional positions rather than additive main effects.

交叉性研究不能只加入一个`gender × race`交互项；必须说明制度如何使身份共同作用，并用样本、测量和案例材料支持独特处境。Intersectional research requires more than a `gender × race` interaction; it must explain how institutions make identities operate jointly and support that explanation with appropriate samples, measures, and cases.

## 11. 混合治理、制度逻辑与第三方 | Hybrid Governance, Institutional Logics, and Third Parties

### 11.1 混合治理 | Hybrid Governance

| 治理逻辑 / Logic | 协调方式 / Coordination | 正当性 / Legitimacy | AI治理表现 / Expression in AI governance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 层级 / Hierarchy | 法律、命令、监督 / law, command, supervision | 公共利益、合法授权 / public interest, lawful authority | 规则、审计、制裁 / rules, audits, sanctions |
| 市场 / Market | 合同、价格、竞争 / contracts, price, competition | 效率、创新、利润 / efficiency, innovation, profit | 模型、云服务、咨询商 / models, cloud services, consultants |
| 网络 / Network | 协商、伙伴、互赖 / negotiation, partnership, interdependence | 共同问题解决 / joint problem solving | 多方标准、联盟、民间参与 / multistakeholder standards, alliances, civil-society participation |

**中文**：混合治理使角色、身份、权力和问责边界重新组合。功能不确定性可为试验保留空间，也可能长期模糊责任。分析时必须问谁决定、谁出资源、谁承担风险、谁能被追责。

**English**: Hybrid governance recombines roles, identities, power, and accountability. Functional indetermination may preserve room for experimentation while leaving responsibility ambiguous. Analysis must identify who decides, supplies resources, bears risk, and can be held to account.

### 11.2 第三方治理与连接型社会资本 | Third-Party Governance and Linking Social Capital

**中文**：非营利组织、学校、商家、保险基金和平台可翻译规则、准备材料、转介与倡导，连接公民与正式机构。但第三方也可能难找、能力不均、彼此竞争、推卸责任，或成为兑换负担来源。数量不等于协调质量。

**English**: Nonprofits, schools, merchants, insurance funds, and platforms can translate rules, prepare documents, refer, and advocate, thereby linking citizens to formal institutions. Yet third parties may be difficult to find, uneven in capacity, competitive, responsibility-shifting, or themselves a source of redemption burden. Density is not coordination quality.

### 11.3 who–what–how减负类型学 | Who–What–How Typology of Deburdening

1. **Who / 分配**：义务转给政府或雇主，还是公民继续承担但获得帮助？Is obligation transferred to government or employers, or shared while citizens remain responsible?
2. **What / 强度**：要求被删除、简化、加速还是推定？Are requirements removed, simplified, accelerated, or presumed satisfied?
3. **How / 关系**：沟通、自主与尊严是否改善？Are communication, autonomy, and dignity improved?

数字化不是独立的第八类策略，而是多种减负策略的实现工具，也可能重新转移成本。Digitalization is not a separate eighth strategy; it enables several strategies and can also transfer costs.

## 12. 设计科学、战略前瞻与用户中心设计 | Design Science, Strategic Foresight, and User-Centered Design

### 12.1 设计科学 | Design Science

**中文**：设计科学以解决现实问题的工件为主要产出，典型循环是问题识别→目标→设计开发→展示→评价→沟通→迭代。工件可为流程、原型、规则或系统。

**English**: Design science produces an artifact that addresses a practical problem. Its cycle typically runs from problem identification to objectives, design and development, demonstration, evaluation, communication, and iteration. An artifact may be a process, prototype, rule, or system.

评价至少包括 / Evaluation should include:

- 功能、效率与质量 / function, efficiency, and quality;
- 真实使用、绕开与修改 / actual use, workarounds, and modification;
- 来源、提示、模型版本与人工改动的可审计性 / auditability of sources, prompts, model versions, and human edits;
- 最终决定是否改善，而非只增加输出数量 / improvement in final decisions, not merely output volume.

### 12.2 战略前瞻 | Strategic Foresight

**中文**：战略前瞻不预测唯一未来，而是识别驱动力、不确定性和多个可行未来，用情景测试战略稳健性。机器适合扫描和组合，人类负责问题边界、可信来源、因果合理性、地方知识、价值冲突和行动含义。

**English**: Strategic foresight does not predict one future. It identifies drivers, uncertainties, and multiple plausible futures and uses scenarios to test strategic robustness. Machines can scan and combine information; humans must define boundaries and assess sources, causal plausibility, local knowledge, value conflict, and action implications.

### 12.3 用户中心设计 | User-Centered Design

`发现具体摩擦 → 映射理论构念 → 设计对应干预 → 观察行为漏斗`  
`Identify concrete friction → map it to a theoretical construct → design a targeted intervention → observe the behavioral funnel`

**证据边界 / Evidence boundary**：点击位于漏斗顶端。完整评估还要跟踪理解、咨询、申请、完成、服务质量和最终结果，并检查平台算法是否改变受众分配。Clicks sit at the top of the funnel. Full evaluation should follow comprehension, inquiry, application, completion, service quality, and final outcomes while examining platform allocation.

## 13. 数字时代国家—公民界面 | The Citizen–State Interface in the Digital Era

### 13.1 零官僚与隐形政府 | Zero Bureaucracy and Invisible Government

| 服务属性 / Service attribute | 中文解释 | English definition |
|---|---|---|
| 一站式 / One-stop | 按生活事件整合多个机构服务 | Integrates services from multiple agencies around life events |
| 主动服务 / Proactive | 政府主动通知、预填或发放 | Government initiates notification, pre-filling, or delivery |
| 一次提交 / Once-only | 公民只提供一次数据，机构依法复用 | Citizens provide data once and agencies lawfully reuse it |
| 虚实渠道 / Virtual and physical channels | 线上、实体或混合接触 | Online, in-person, or hybrid contact |

**中文**：零官僚与隐形政府是理想型，不意味着政府真的消失。后台数据匹配、资格判断、纠错和责任反而更加重要。

**English**: Zero bureaucracy and invisible government are ideal types; the state does not literally disappear. Backend matching, eligibility determination, error correction, and responsibility become more important.

### 13.2 减负与新负担的竞争链条 | Competing Chains of Burden Reduction and New Burden

`一站式／主动／一次提交 → 搜索与重复证明减少 → 学习和遵从成本下降`  
`One-stop/proactive/once-only service → less search and repeated proof → lower learning and compliance costs`

`数据整合／虚拟化 → 隐私、误配、排斥与申诉困难 → 新心理与纠错成本`  
`Data integration/virtualization → privacy, mismatching, exclusion, and appeal barriers → new psychological and correction costs`

偏好实验只能说明公众偏好某些属性，不能证明真实系统降低负担。Preference experiments show how citizens value attributes; they do not establish actual burden reduction.

## 14. 监管治理、节奏问题与政策能力 | Regulatory Governance, the Pacing Problem, and Policy Capacity

### 14.1 节奏问题与适应性工具 | The Pacing Problem and Adaptive Tools

**中文**：技术变化可能快于立法、监管知识与执行能力。适应性工具包括监管沙盒、测试床、实验规则、日落条款、技术标准和定期审查。目的不是放松监管，而是在不确定性下降时更新规则。

**English**: Technological change may outpace legislation, regulatory knowledge, and enforcement capacity. Adaptive tools include regulatory sandboxes, test beds, experimental rules, sunset clauses, technical standards, and periodic review. Their purpose is not simply deregulation but rule updating as uncertainty declines.

### 14.2 政策能力 | Policy Capacity

| 能力 / Capacity | 核心内容 / Core content |
|---|---|
| 分析 / Analytical | 理解技术、风险、数据和因果证据 / understanding technology, risk, data, and causal evidence |
| 操作 / Operational | 制定标准、检查、协调、配置人员与学习 / setting standards, inspecting, coordinating, staffing, and learning |
| 政治 / Political | 建立合法性、处理冲突、沟通并维持支持 / building legitimacy, managing conflict, communicating, and sustaining support |

能力存在个人、组织和系统层级。财政只是能力的一部分；不采纳也可能来自政治偏好、法律不确定或目标群体权力不足。Capacity exists at individual, organizational, and system levels. Fiscal resources are only one component; nonadoption may also reflect political preferences, legal uncertainty, or weak target-group power.

### 14.3 风险清单怎样变成治理 | Turning a Risk List into Governance

`风险识别 → 责任主体 → 预防控制 → 监测指标 → 触发阈值 → 纠正／停止 → 规则更新`  
`Risk identification → responsible actor → preventive control → monitoring indicator → trigger threshold → correction/termination → rule update`

文件中出现标准只能说明正式覆盖，不能说明合规与安全效果。A standard appearing in a document establishes formal coverage, not compliance or safety outcomes.

## 15. 重要的作者自建框架 | Important Author-Developed Frameworks

### 15.1 Halling与Baekgaard：行政负担因果地图 | Halling and Baekgaard: Administrative-Burden Causal Map

**中文**：贡献在于连接国家行动、执行接触、体验、结果、负担容忍和政策反馈。每条箭头必须分别评估证据。相对薄弱的箭头，例如体验怎样改变民主行为、容忍怎样转化为政策，是比“文献还不够”更具体的研究空白。

**English**: The map connects state action, implementation encounters, experiences, outcomes, burden tolerance, and policy feedback. Evidence must be evaluated arrow by arrow. Weak links, such as how experiences change democratic behavior or tolerance becomes policy, provide more precise gaps than claiming that the literature is simply insufficient.

### 15.2 Roehl与Hansen：善治价值矩阵 | Roehl and Hansen: Good-Governance Value Matrix

**中文**：九项价值和协同、权衡、边界三类关系用于研究前的价值审计。必须区分文献提出的可能性与现实中直接测量的价值结果，并说明价值由谁定义。

**English**: Nine values and three relationship types, synergy, trade-off, and limit, support a pre-study value audit. Researchers must separate possibilities discussed in the literature from directly measured outcomes and identify whose definition of value is used.

### 15.3 Campbell等：五个元叙事 | Campbell et al.: Five Metanarratives

**中文**：行政绩效、行为影响、规则质量、公民获得和行政体系政治化是观察规则的五个入口，不是互斥变量。它们允许同一规则被放入组织、公民和政治层次同时分析。

**English**: Administrative performance, behavioral effects, rule quality, citizen access, and politicization are five entry points for studying rules, not mutually exclusive variables. They support multilevel analysis of the same rule across organizations, citizens, and politics.

### 15.4 Zhu：with / of / through AI | Zhu: Public Administration with, of, and through AI

- `with AI`：AI作为行政工具 / AI as an administrative tool.
- `of AI`：AI作为治理对象 / AI as an object of governance.
- `through AI`：AI作为研究工具和认识方式 / AI as a research tool and mode of knowledge production.

三者相互约束：实践能力需要治理边界，治理需要技术知识，研究工具需要公共价值和伦理。The three constrain one another: practical capacity requires governance boundaries, governance requires technical knowledge, and research tools require public-value and ethical constraints.

### 15.5 Kim与Nam：条件性信任 | Kim and Nam: Conditional Trust

`感知重要性 + 对回答的信任 + 支持文化 − 操作担忧 → 实际使用`  
`Perceived importance + trust in responses + supportive culture − operational concerns → actual use`

**中文**：这是调节模型，不是简单信任中介。未来应加入任务错误成本、正式授权、数字素养与可复核性，并用日志和纵向设计区分使用产生信任与信任产生使用。

**English**: This is a moderation model, not a simple trust-mediation model. Future work should incorporate task error costs, formal authorization, digital literacy, and reviewability and use logs and longitudinal designs to distinguish use-generated trust from trust-generated use.

### 15.6 Vogl等：算法官僚 | Vogl et al.: Algorithmic Bureaucracy

**中文**：该中层概念把分析单位从单个官僚改为由前线人员、数据、模型、设计者、供应商与管理者组成的决策网络。可操作指标包括裁量位置、人工介入点、模型更新权、异常升级、供应商依赖、责任分配和申诉接点。

**English**: This middle-range concept shifts the unit of analysis from an individual bureaucrat to a decision network of frontline workers, data, models, designers, vendors, and managers. Operational indicators include the location of discretion, human intervention points, model-update authority, exception escalation, vendor dependence, responsibility, and appeal points.

### 15.7 Benish等：who–what–how | Benish et al.: Who–What–How

**中文**：类型学应按类别清晰、相对穷尽、能区分案例并产生比较命题来评价。策略可重叠，应使用多标签编码而不是强迫互斥分类。

**English**: A typology should be judged by category clarity, relative exhaustiveness, case differentiation, and its ability to generate comparative propositions. Strategies may overlap and should be coded with multiple labels rather than forced into exclusive categories.

### 15.8 Hegemann与Ritz：数字负担TOI | Hegemann and Ritz: TOI Model of Digital Burden

**中文**：负担是系统配置结果，而不是公民点击页面后才产生。后台互操作、组织分工和数据规则预先决定重复证明、错误修正与人工帮助。研究单位可为服务流程或技术—组织—群体配置。

**English**: Burden is an outcome of system configuration rather than an experience beginning only when a citizen opens a webpage. Backend interoperability, organizational division of labor, and data rules prestructure repeated proof, error correction, and human assistance. The unit of analysis may be a service process or a technology–organization–group configuration.

## 16. 从理论生成研究问题、假设与框架 | From Theory to Research Questions, Hypotheses, and Frameworks

### 16.1 先写清楚要解释的Y | Begin with the Outcome Y

| 结果Y / Outcome | 优先理论 / Candidate theories |
|---|---|
| 组织为什么采用AI / Why organizations adopt AI | TOE、公私差异、创新扩散、合法性 / TOE, public–private differences, diffusion, legitimacy |
| 官员是否持续使用GenAI / Whether officials continue using GenAI | TAM、信任、组织文化、任务风险 / TAM, trust, culture, task risk |
| AI怎样改变官僚角色 / How AI changes bureaucratic roles | 街头/系统层官僚、实践理论、组织设计 / street-/system-level bureaucracy, practice theory, organizational design |
| 公民是否接受自动决定 / Whether citizens accept automated decisions | 公共价值、代表性、算法厌恶、程序公平 / public value, representation, algorithm aversion, procedural fairness |
| 数字服务是否造成负担 / Whether digital service creates burden | 行政负担、TOI、人力资本、行政素养 / administrative burden, TOI, human capital, literacy |
| 地方为什么减负 / Why localities deburden | 政策能力、扩散、目标群体建构、政治注意 / policy capacity, diffusion, target-group construction, political attention |
| AI治理为什么公私混合 / Why AI governance is hybrid | 混合治理、制度逻辑、第三方 / hybrid governance, institutional logics, third parties |
| 规则如何追赶技术 / How regulation catches technology | 监管治理、节奏问题、政策能力 / regulatory governance, pacing problem, policy capacity |

### 16.2 画最小因果图 | Draw the Minimum Causal Diagram

先画 / Begin with:  
`X（制度／技术／信息） → M（理论机制） → Y（结果）`  
`X (institution/technology/information) → M (theoretical mechanism) → Y (outcome)`

只加入一个必要条件 / Add one necessary condition:  
`W（能力／文化／任务风险）调节X→M或M→Y`  
`W (capacity/culture/task risk) moderates X→M or M→Y`

例子 / Example:  
`主动服务 → 学习与遵从成本下降 → 申请完成率上升`  
`Proactive service → lower learning and compliance costs → higher application completion`

`数字素养调节主动服务→成本下降`  
`Digital literacy moderates proactive service → cost reduction`

不要把十个理论堆成“意大利面图”。每加入一个构念，都应说明是否可测、为何因果上必要、样本是否支持。Do not begin with a spaghetti model containing ten theories. For each construct, ask whether it is measurable, causally necessary, and supportable with the available sample.

### 16.3 将构念操作化 | Operationalize Theoretical Constructs

| 构念 / Construct | 弱代理 / Weak proxy | 更好的组合 / Better measurement combination |
|---|---|---|
| 行政负担 / Administrative burden | “手续麻烦吗？” / “Is the process troublesome?” | 客观步骤、等待、文件 + 三类成本 + 完成/退出 / objective steps, waiting, documents + three costs + completion/exit |
| 支持文化 / Supportive culture | 一道领导支持题 / one leadership item | 政策、培训、资源、同侪规范、错误容忍、日志 / policy, training, resources, peer norms, error tolerance, logs |
| 信任 / Trust | 总体好感 / general favorability | 能力、完整性、可预测性、制度保障、任务依赖 / ability, integrity, predictability, safeguards, task-specific reliance |
| 公平 / Fairness | “公平吗？” / “Is it fair?” | 分配、程序、互动、纠正 + 群体结果 / distributive, procedural, interactional, corrective + group outcomes |
| 政策能力 / Policy capacity | 财政收入 / fiscal revenue | 分析人员、预算、数据、协调、执行、学习 / analysts, budget, data, coordination, implementation, learning |
| 裁量 / Discretion | 是否有人参与 / any human involvement | 决策点、范围、修正权、记录、复核、问责 / decision points, scope, revision authority, records, review, accountability |
| 第三方 / Third parties | 组织数量 / organization count | 合同、服务量、转介、能力、覆盖、网络与质量 / contracts, service volume, referrals, capacity, coverage, networks, quality |

### 16.4 理论命题与识别设计对齐 | Align Theory with Identification

- “信息框架改变态度”适合随机情境实验。A randomized vignette fits a claim about information framing and attitudes.
- “政策实施改变真实参与”需要行政数据、DID、RDD或随机上线。A claim about implementation and real participation requires administrative data, DID, RDD, or randomized rollout.
- “组织成员重构技术”适合深度案例、观察和过程追踪。A claim about organizational enactment fits cases, observation, and process tracing.
- “X通过M影响Y”需要安排M的时间与测量，最好操纵或多时点观察。A mediation claim requires temporal measurement of M, ideally manipulation or multiple waves.
- “效果取决于W”需要预先明确交互方向、范围、共同支持和功效。A moderation claim requires a prespecified direction, range, common support, and power.

方法复杂不等于理论贴合。横截面SEM不能证明时间顺序；文字实验不能证明制度长期效果。Methodological complexity is not theoretical alignment. Cross-sectional SEM does not establish temporal order, and a text experiment does not demonstrate long-term institutional effects.

### 16.5 预先写竞争解释 | Specify Competing Explanations in Advance

若AI依赖与努力正相关，至少考虑 / If AI dependence is positively associated with effort, consider:

- 反向因果：更努力的人更常用工具 / reverse causality: more diligent employees use the tool more;
- 共同原因：技术自信同时提高使用与努力 / common cause: technological confidence affects both;
- 选择：只调查使用者 / selection: only users enter the sample;
- 社会期许：同时高报使用与努力 / social desirability: both use and effort are overreported.

研究设计的任务是削弱竞争解释，而不是在显著后补故事。The purpose of research design is to weaken competing explanations, not to add a story after significance appears.

### 16.6 定义什么算支持理论 | Define What Counts as Support

在分析前说明 / Specify before analysis:

- 主效应方向和实质大小 / direction and substantive size;
- 中介或调节是否必须出现 / whether mediation or moderation is required;
- 哪些结果会反驳理论 / what would contradict the theory;
- 多重结果与多重检验 / multiple outcomes and testing;
- 支持完整理论、某条命题还是特定情境 / whether support applies to the full theory, one proposition, or one setting.

`p < .05`不是理论支持的充分条件。`p < .05` is not a sufficient condition for theoretical support.

## 17. 六条跨论文研究路线 | Six Cross-Paper Research Routes

### 路线一：从AI采用到制度化使用 | Route 1: From AI Adoption to Institutionalized Use

`TOE阶段条件 → 支持文化与正式授权 → 校准信任 → 任务级使用 → 学习与绩效`  
`Stage-specific TOE conditions → supportive culture and authorization → calibrated trust → task-level use → learning and performance`

对应 / Papers: Neumann et al.; Kim & Nam; Wilson & Broomfield; Mergel et al.  
适合 / Best suited to: 纵向组织研究 / longitudinal organizational research.

### 路线二：AI怎样产生或减少行政负担 | Route 2: How AI Creates or Reduces Administrative Burden

`技术／组织设计（TOI） → 街头与系统层裁量 → 三类负担 → 参与与公平`  
`Technological/organizational design (TOI) → street- and system-level discretion → three costs → participation and equity`

对应 / Papers: Hegemann & Ritz; Vogl et al.; Aoki; Halling & Baekgaard; Bell & Meyer.  
数据要求 / Data requirement: 后台系统 + 一线工作 + 公民结果 / backend system + frontline work + citizen outcomes.

### 路线三：人机协作何时是增强 | Route 3: When Human–AI Collaboration Is Augmentation

`任务与组织设计 → 人机任务分配 → 自我效能／裁量／认知负荷 → 努力与质量`  
`Task and organizational design → human–AI task allocation → self-efficacy/discretion/cognitive load → effort and quality`

对应 / Papers: Maragno et al.; Zhang et al.; Picavet et al.; Giest & Klievink.  
证据要求 / Evidence requirement: 任务流与客观绩效，不只态度 / task flows and objective performance, not attitudes alone.

### 路线四：公众为什么接受政府AI | Route 4: Why Citizens Accept Government AI

`代表性与经历 + 部署／披露 + 公平／隐私／效用判断 → 信任、合法性和使用`  
`Representation and experience + deployment/disclosure + equity/privacy/utility judgments → trust, legitimacy, and use`

对应 / Papers: Miller & Keiser; Keppeler; Willems et al.; Roehl & Hansen; Busuioc.  
可操纵 / Possible manipulations: 人工参与、解释、申诉、公平审计、数据使用 / human involvement, explanation, appeal, fairness audit, data use.

### 路线五：减负为什么具有政治分配性 | Route 5: Why Deburdening Is Politically Distributive

`目标群体／意识形态 + 政策能力与注意 → 减负选择 → 群体实际成本`  
`Target-group construction/ideology + policy capacity and attention → deburdening choice → group-specific costs`

对应 / Papers: Bell et al.; Zhou & Mast; both Yang papers; Herd & Moynihan; Benish et al.  
连接 / Link: 制定者认知、文本、实施记录与群体结果 / policymaker beliefs, texts, implementation records, and group outcomes.

### 路线六：从AI原则到可问责治理 | Route 6: From AI Principles to Accountable Governance

`制度逻辑 → 权责配置 → 风险标准与试验 → 三阶段问责 → 规则更新`  
`Institutional logics → allocation of authority and responsibility → risk standards and experimentation → three-stage accountability → rule updating`

对应 / Papers: Radu; Pande & Taeihagh; Busuioc; Henman; Zhu.  
适合 / Best suited to: 国家、采购模式或监管沙盒比较 / comparisons across countries, procurement models, or regulatory sandboxes.

## 18. 十个常见理论错误 | Ten Common Theoretical Errors

1. **提到理论就写成“基于理论” / Treating mention as grounding.** 核心构念必须进入问题与分析。Core constructs must enter the question and analysis.
2. **把框架当因果理论 / Treating a framework as causal theory.** TOE、TOI和价值矩阵首先是分类工具。TOE, TOI, and value matrices are primarily organizing tools.
3. **把统计中介当机制证明 / Treating statistical mediation as mechanism proof.** 横截面路径不能建立时间顺序。Cross-sectional paths do not establish temporal order.
4. **把交互不显著写成没有差异 / Equating a nonsignificant interaction with no difference.** 还要检查功效、范围、共同支持与置信区间。Check power, range, common support, and confidence intervals.
5. **把概念例子当总体证据 / Treating illustrations as population evidence.** 例子表明概念可识别，不表明发生率。Examples show recognizability, not prevalence.
6. **理论堆砌 / Theory stacking.** 每套理论必须对应一条必要箭头。Each theory must explain a necessary arrow.
7. **用控制变量冒充理论 / Treating controls as theory.** 加入性别或年龄不等于使用性别或生命周期理论。Including gender or age is not using gender or life-course theory.
8. **把态度当行为 / Treating attitudes as behavior.** 支持、意愿、点击、部署与申领是不同结果。Support, intentions, clicks, deployment, and take-up are distinct outcomes.
9. **把效率当公共价值总和 / Treating efficiency as total public value.** 还要考虑公平、问责、法治、隐私与回应。Equity, accountability, rule of law, privacy, and responsiveness also matter.
10. **结果后编机制 / Inventing mechanisms after results.** 合理故事必须标为解释，并提出直接测量。Plausible stories should be labeled as interpretations and followed by direct measurement proposals.

## 19. 三轮学习顺序 | Three-Pass Learning Sequence

### 第一轮：共同语言 | Pass 1: Common Language

掌握行政负担三成本与红带区别；街头、系统层和算法官僚；TOE、TOI与TAM的层次；公共价值、问责与行政正义；代表性、目标群体与交叉性。Master the three burden costs and red tape distinction; street-, system-level, and algorithmic bureaucracy; the levels addressed by TOE, TOI, and TAM; public value, accountability, and administrative justice; representation, target groups, and intersectionality.

### 第二轮：每篇四句话 | Pass 2: Four Sentences per Paper

1. 采用什么理论或框架？What theory or framework is used?
2. 最小因果链或分类维度是什么？What is the minimum causal chain or classification?
3. 哪个构念被操纵或测量？Which construct is manipulated or measured?
4. 属于直接检验、部分支持、解释性使用还是未检验？Is use direct, partial, interpretive, or untested?

### 第三轮：反向设计 | Pass 3: Reverse-Engineer a Study

- 换国家或政策领域，哪个条件会变？Which condition changes in another country or policy domain?
- 哪个关键中介没有测？Which key mediator is unmeasured?
- 哪个竞争理论也能解释？Which competing theory also fits?
- 什么数据或识别设计能区分？What data or identification strategy can distinguish them?
- 影响哪项公共价值，存在什么权衡？Which public value is affected, and what trade-off follows?

## 21. 最后自测 | Final Self-Test

如果能够不看原文完成以下五项，才算真正掌握理论。You have learned a theory when you can complete all five tasks without consulting the source:

1. 用两句话说明理论解释的结果与核心机制。State the outcome and core mechanism in two sentences.
2. 画出最小因果链并指出边界条件。Draw the minimum causal chain and identify a boundary condition.
3. 为每个构念提出可观察测量。Propose an observable measure for every construct.
4. 指出哪篇论文直接检验、哪篇只作解释性使用。Identify which paper tests the theory and which uses it interpretively.
5. 提出能区分该理论与竞争解释的研究设计。Propose a design that distinguishes the theory from a competing explanation.

本手册应与[研究方法与数据处理学习手册](METHODS_AND_DATA_GUIDE_CN.md)配合：理论解释为什么预期有关系，方法解释数据和设计能把证据推到多远。Use this guide with the [methods and data guide](METHODS_AND_DATA_GUIDE_CN.md): theory explains why a relationship is expected, while methods determine how far the data and design can support it.
